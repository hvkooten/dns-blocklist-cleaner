# HaGeZi DNS Blocklists – Help

**Cleans the Internet and protects your privacy!**

An all-in-one DNS blocklist available in several versions (Light, Normal, Pro,
Pro++ and Ultimate). It blocks ads, affiliate, tracking, metrics, telemetry,
phishing, malware, scam, fake and other "crap". The lists are optimized to avoid
false positives as much as possible without losing effectiveness.

This tool downloads a HaGeZi **Wildcard Domains** list and lets you remove
entries with your own rules before generating a cleaned file.

---

## Which version should I use?

| Version | Blocking level | Blocking type |
|:--------|:---------------|:--------------|
| <b style="color:#3ba55d">Light</b> | Relaxed | Minimal blocking – blocks ads, tracking and metrics. Size-optimized. |
| <b style="color:#3b82c4">Normal</b> | Relaxed / Balanced | All-round protection. Adds phishing, malware, scam, fake, cryptojacking. |
| <b style="color:#d4a017">Pro</b> | Balanced | **Recommended.** Extended protection with good privacy and few restrictions. |
| <b style="color:#e8590c">Pro++</b> | Balanced / Aggressive | Maximum protection, more aggressive. For experienced users. |
| <b style="color:#e03131">Ultimate</b> | Aggressive | Strictest list. May limit some apps/sites. For experienced users. |
